# gfx906 MMQ Q8_0 Thread/Tile Sweep: 2026-08-07

## Purpose

Test 512-thread Q8_0 MMQ configs on MI50 (gfx906) after the I=64 sweep on
2026-08-06, per the handoff plan. The 256-thread Q4_K/Q5_K/Q6_K overrides from
the production patch are preserved throughout.

## Controlled configuration

- llama.cpp: `360e1349f0009c5ad99d21e3c4546b707addc68a`
- ROCm 7.14, LLVM 23, target `gfx906`
- Compiler flag: `-mllvm -amdgpu-sched-strategy=max-ilp`
- Device: one MI50, `ROCm0`
- `--n-prompt 2048 --ubatch-size 2048 --batch-size 2048 --n-gen 256`
- `--n-depth 0 --split-mode layer --flash-attn on`
- Models: Qwen3.5-9B Q8_0 and Gemma-4-26B A4B Q4_K_L

## Variants

| Variant | Q8_0 threads | Occupancy | I | Exposed J | Notes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| production | 256 | 2 | 64 | 64 | current patch |
| A | 512 | 1 | 128 | 64 only | handoff first candidate |
| B | 512 | 2 | 128 | 64 only | occupancy 2 control |
| D | 512 | 1 | 128 | 128 only | wide tile |
| F | 512 | 2 | 128 | 8..128 | mx-llama.cpp style, occupancy gate, MXFP4 512 |

Variants C (256/2/128/128) and E (512/2/128/128) were skipped: A vs B showed
occupancy does not change resource allocation (identical VGPR/SGPR/scratch), so
C adds nothing and E is expected to equal D.

## Gate behavior (variant F)

`mul_mat_q_switch_J` occupancy gate, ported from mx-llama.cpp, holds J<=64 for
MoE (`ids_dst != nullptr`) and for grids that cannot fill the CUs
(`total_tiles < nsm`).

Verified on Gemma Q4_K_L (MoE): Q8_0 uses J=128 for dense ops (414 calls,
0.590 s) and J=64 for MoE experts (84 calls, 0.528 s). Q4_K/Q5_K/Q6_K keep
J=64. Tensor-sharded Qwen PP: 989.74, with J=128 for full rows and J=64 for
the fallback edge rows.

## Conclusion

Variant F is the best candidate. It improves Qwen PP by `+34.1%` and Gemma PP
by `+3.9%` with no TG regression, keeps Q8_0 at 89 VGPR / 0 scratch, and the
occupancy gate protects MoE and row-sharded shapes from the wide J=128 tile.
